# When an outside service is down

MandiMangoes leans on nine outside services. This page says, for each one, what
customers see when it stops answering, what the system does by itself, how staff
find out, and what to do. The behaviour is taken from the code (files named in
brackets). The steps are in [RUNBOOKS.md](RUNBOOKS.md).

First check which one it is: `curl -s https://api.mandimangoes.in/health` (the API
runs) and `/ready` (the database and the every-minute job), then the provider's
status page.

| Service | What customers see | What the system does | How staff notice | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Razorpay API** (orders, refunds, payment checks) | Paying fails: "Could not start the payment. Please try again." (502 `PAYMENT_GATEWAY_ERROR`, after a 10 s timeout). Browsing is not slowed down. | The order is cancelled and its boxes go back on sale; the next try is a fresh order (`customer.js` `startPayment`). Refunds that fail stay owed and the every-minute job sends them again (`payments.js` `processDueRefunds`). | Sentry errors `could not create the gateway order`; refunds showing in **Needs attention**; customers saying they cannot pay. | Check status.razorpay.com. Nothing to undo: no money moved. Once it is back, check [Stuck or failed refunds](RUNBOOKS.md#stuck-or-failed-refunds). |
| **Razorpay webhooks** (Razorpay telling us a payment went through) | Nothing, or the order shows "confirming payment" for a minute or so longer. | The every-minute job asks Razorpay about orders still waiting for payment, before the sweeper releases any holds, so a paid order is confirmed, not cancelled (`payments.js` `reconcilePendingPayments`). The shop also asks when the customer comes back from Checkout (`reconcileOrderNow`). Razorpay retries any webhook we did not accept. | Sentry "Payments were confirmed by polling". | [Webhooks not arriving](RUNBOOKS.md#webhooks-not-arriving), or [Webhooks failing (500)](RUNBOOKS.md#webhooks-failing-500). |
| **Meta WhatsApp** | Order updates (confirmed, out for delivery, delivery code) arrive late or not at all. Login codes come by SMS instead. Ordering in the WhatsApp chat stops answering. | Each message is kept in the outbox and retried at 1, 5, 15, 60 and 180 minutes, then skipped after 24 hours (`notify.js`). Login codes fall back to SMS (`sms.js`). | **Needs attention** "WhatsApp message not delivered"; Sentry "WhatsApp messages failing" or "overdue"; Sentry "Login codes are going out on the backup channel". | [Failed notifications](RUNBOOKS.md#failed-notifications). Riders use a handover photo when a customer has no delivery code. |
| **Jio SMS** | Login codes come by WhatsApp instead. A customer without WhatsApp cannot sign in by phone ("We could not send the code", 502 `OTP_NOT_SENT`); Google sign-in still works. | Each code is tried on the other channel after the first fails (`sms.js`). | Sentry "Login codes are going out on the backup channel" (5 in an hour) or "Login codes are not being sent" (3 in 10 minutes). | [Login codes not arriving](RUNBOOKS.md#login-codes-not-arriving). |
| **Google sign-in** | "Sign in with Google is not answering just now. Please try again in a minute." (503 `GOOGLE_UNAVAILABLE`). Phone sign-in works. Staff sign in with their password and two-step code. | Nothing to catch up: no account is changed. Google's keys are fetched again on the next try (`google.js`). | Customers saying Google sign-in fails; Vercel logs "Google's sign-in keys could not be fetched". | Wait for Google (status.cloud.google.com). Tell customers to use their phone number meanwhile. |
| **Neon database** | "We are having trouble right now. Please try again in a minute." (503 `UNAVAILABLE`) or "We are very busy" (503 `BUSY`) on every page that needs data. | Nothing is half-written: each change is one transaction. Razorpay retries its webhooks; holds that ran out are released and missed payments confirmed by the first job run after it is back. | Uptime monitor on `/ready` (503); Sentry "database unavailable" (once a minute at most). | [Downtime or database outage](RUNBOOKS.md#downtime-or-database-outage). |
| **Vercel** (API, shop and panel hosting, the every-minute job) | The shop and the panel do not load. | Nothing runs. Razorpay keeps retrying its webhooks (for about a day). When Vercel is back, the first job run releases stale holds after confirming any payments that came in, and sends queued messages. | Uptime monitor on `/ready` and the shop; vercel-status.com; Sentry missed cron check-ins. | [Downtime or database outage](RUNBOOKS.md#downtime-or-database-outage) step 1, then wait. Afterwards [Scheduler stopped](RUNBOOKS.md#scheduler-stopped) if `/ready` stays 503. |
| **Vercel Blob** (photos) | Product, deal and review photos do not load. A rider cannot save a handover photo (use the delivery code instead). A customer cannot attach a photo to a problem report. | Each Blob call gives up after 15 s with `PROVIDER_CHECKS` on (`photos.js`). Nothing is lost that was saved: rows point at the photo's path. A photo that failed to save is not attached to anything. Photos have **no copy outside Blob** (see [VERCEL.md](VERCEL.md#backups-and-restore-drill)). | Sentry errors on photo routes; riders and customers reporting failed uploads. | Check vercel-status.com. Riders use the delivery code; customers can send a problem report without a photo, or try again later. |
| **Sentry** (error reports) | Nothing. | Errors are still written to the Vercel logs; only the reports and alerts are missing. | Alerts stop arriving; sentry.io status page. | Until it is back, check the panel's **Needs attention** card and Vercel → `mandi-api` → Logs a few times a day. |
